# Brand Identity Guide (Phase 06)

## Brand Name & Voice
- **Brand:** `RIVALRY 90`
- **Primary Tagline:** `THE GAME. THE GLORY. THE RIVALRY.`
- **Voice:** Short, athletic, confident, competitive, and accessible.
- **Originality Policy:** Zero copied FIFA, EA Sports, Konami, or eFootball logos, club crests, or player likenesses.

## SVG Brand Marks (`public/branding/`)
- `logo-primary.svg` — Full horizontal wordmark + badge
- `logo-compact.svg` — Compact header/card mark
- `logo-mark.svg` — Square emblem mark
- `logo-light.svg` / `logo-dark.svg` — Monochrome contrast variants
- `favicon.svg` — Browser tab icon
